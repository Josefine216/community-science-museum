Free entry

It's totally free to come and visit us at the museum, although you are also able to book a guided trip for 70NOK. We want to keep this place available for everybody, because we believe everybody deserves the opportunity to learn about science. You can bring your own food, or buy food and something to drink from the cafeteria. The cafeteria is open all opening hours. You are able to book the guided tour beforehand or when you arrive.
